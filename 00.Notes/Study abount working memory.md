
Working memory is like an GPS. 
- Ela serve para nos ajudar a guiar nosso comportamento em direção a um destino espesifico. 

## Principios 

- **Off-load the content** in working memory on to other storage devices, including paper. 
- **Use stick notes, cards, cues,** and other ways of physically representing information in that setting to help guide their behavior 
- **Digital tools podem ajudar, mas low-tech paper journals & Notes são melhores do que soluções high-tech**
- **Use pictures and imagens about the task** and the goal, to help keep focused on it. (look for picture sequences on the internet for many routine task's)
- **Drawn simple pictures** with arrows connecting them, showing the sequence of steps to follow
