**Judge** = These individuals are Open and confident in their own judgment. They lean toward the idea of "conflict up front for the sake of harmony later." (São abertos e confiam no seu proprio julgamento)

**Jury** = These individuals are Closed and often make decisions primarily based on credibility. They are very resourceful but struggle to trust their own perception.
	São muito resouceful mas lutam para confiar na propria percepção.

**Witness** = These individuals are Closed and among the most peaceful types. They are confident in their perceptions but are often indecisive and tend to defer to others' judgements as long as it's consistent with their perceptions. 
	(Confiam na sua percepção, mas são indecisas com seu julgamento)

**Attorney** = These individuals are Open and often very self-sacrificing, but they lack confidence in their own judgment. As a result, they may struggle to solve their own problems, sacrificing their needs for the sake of others' peace. 
	(São abertos e muito self-sacrificing em prol dos outros) (Sacrificando suas necessidades em prol da paz dos outros)
